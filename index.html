Who we are

Development that starts with people.

We connect community priorities with knowledge, advocacy, partnerships and practical opportunities—especially for people whose voices are often underrepresented.

We believe every child, young person, woman and community deserves the tools, choices and supportive systems to thrive—and a meaningful role in shaping Liberia's future.

Strategic direction

Designed for Liberia.
Connected to national priorities.

GLA's proposed program strategy complements Liberia's ARREST Agenda for Inclusive Development (2025–2029) and Agenda 2030. It is a civil-society contribution—not a government program or official endorsement.

Across counties and communities, we aim to help translate broad priorities into achievable community actions: youth participation, skills, health awareness, entrepreneurship, environmental stewardship, transparent advocacy and partnerships that mobilize local resources.

Human capital

People, skills & essential services

Strengthen community awareness, education pathways, wellbeing and youth capabilities.

  • Promote school participation, mentoring, literacy and practical/digital skills.
  • Support accurate health and reproductive-health information, referrals and service awareness.
  • Prioritize girls, youth, people with disabilities and underserved residents in program design.

Inclusive economy

Livelihoods & local enterprise

Create practical pathways from skills to work and business opportunity.

  • Offer entrepreneurship orientation, business basics, peer learning and mentoring.
  • Connect small enterprises, especially youth- and women-led, to markets and partner networks.
  • Promote value addition, responsible supply chains and livelihood diversification.

Governance & inclusion

Community voice & accountability

Help people participate in decisions affecting their lives and local resources.

  • Convene community dialogues, youth-led forums and public-interest awareness.
  • Promote transparency, inclusion, human rights and constructive engagement.
  • Build a feedback mechanism that records concerns, response and follow-up.

Environment & resilience

Climate-smart community action

Support locally appropriate solutions for environmental pressures and climate risks.

  • Raise awareness on flood risk, waste, coastal/river protection and biodiversity.
  • Support community cleanups, tree and ecosystem activities where appropriate.
  • Advocate for resilient services, sustainable energy and responsible natural-resource practices.

Impact & accountability

We will track progress,
not just activities.

We propose an accessible monitoring approach: agree on baselines, record participation and outputs, collect community feedback, review what is working, and share learning with partners.

Evidence. Community voice. Improvement.

Indicators will be set with participants and partners before activities begin. We will avoid inflated claims and publish results only when verified.
