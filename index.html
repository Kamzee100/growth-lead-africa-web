Who we are

Local voices, lasting progress.

Growth Lead Africa is a community-focused organization in Liberia advancing sustainable development through advocacy, partnership and opportunity.

We believe every young person, woman, girl, boy and child deserves the chance to live well and help shape the future of their community.

Our vision

Communities where people can thrive through education, good health, climate resilience and inclusive economic growth.

Our mission

To create opportunities and advocate for quality education, healthcare, social growth and sustainable livelihoods through strong partnerships.

Who we serve

Our work centers the people closest to the challenges and the solutions.
